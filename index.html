Innovating from the perspective of manual capability means that exhaust ventilation products no longer pursue only air volume; instead, they focus on how people turn them on and off, adjust them, clean them, and handle emergencies. It shifts the relationship from “people adapting to machines” to “machines adapting to people,” turning manual operation from a burden into an optional and controllable mode of interaction.

In short, the significance of innovation in household exhaust ventilation products lies in compensating for and enhancing people’s manual capabilities through automation, ease of use, safety, and accessibility design, reducing unnecessary physical exertion and skill demands while retaining reliable manual control. This not only improves convenience and health in daily life but also drives exhaust products toward more human-centered and sustainable upgrading.
